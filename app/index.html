The Leonida Field Manual

Every trophy, every step, every source. A complete offline guide and tracker for the 55 trophies of GTA VI — built from the official list, cited throughout, with the missables flagged before they can bite.

How this manual was built

Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. Every guide is written in our own words for this manual and cites the sources it was built from; a source is named beside each guide, never copied. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is shown and a call is made in the open. This app runs entirely offline from its own files — it fetches nothing, and the only links out are the cited sources, opened only when the reader follows them. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, box art or game screenshot appears anywhere in it; the look is our own.

Progress is kept in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing leaves the device.
